DESIGN (as agreed)
- A link box may sit at any nesting depth. One box per level, max 4 rows
  per box (MAX_LINK_CHAIN unchanged), MAX_DEPTH unchanged at 4.
- Followers sit directly under their mother at the SAME depth; the mother's
  nested child is the first row AFTER the box. Followers are leaves (never
  have children).
- Per step of a level: level write/ramp/verify -> followers top-to-bottom
  -> level Integration Time -> recurse / measure. Identical ordering to the
  single-level link box.
- Data file: after each level's {level}_count, {level}_value come that
  level's {alias}_value columns. Chains without links produce the exact
  same files as before.
